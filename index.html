Our story

It began with 950 days on a bicycle.

Founder Jyotishko Biswas spent nearly 950 days cycling across India, completing over 35 treks through some of the country's most untouched trails. During the COVID years, a small group of explorers followed him into the pristine forests of Meghalaya — and a modest $20 tip for guiding revealed something bigger: people were searching for authentic, well-guided trekking rooted in knowledge and responsibility.

  1. 950 daysCycling across India · 35+ treks on foot
  2. TrainingHimalayan Mountaineering Institute & NIMAS
  3. InstructorTaught aspiring mountaineers at HMI, Darjeeling
  4. 2021Trekking Buddha is born — offbeat, skill-based, responsible
